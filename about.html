Our Story

Built on Trust & Results

Great Properties GA, LLC is Georgia's premier real estate investment and redevelopment company, dedicated to providing fast, fair solutions for homeowners across the state.

We specialize in buying properties in any condition — from move-in ready homes to distressed properties that need significant work. Our mission: provide homeowners with a stress-free alternative to the traditional real estate process while creating value in the communities we serve.

Unlike traditional agents who simply list your property, we are direct buyers. No waiting months for a buyer, no costly repairs, no agent commissions, no uncertain closing dates. When we make you an offer, we mean it — and we close on your timeline.
