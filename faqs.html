Frequently Asked Questions

How much will you offer for my house?+
Our offers are based on the property's location, condition, and recent comparable sales in the area. We make fair, data-driven offers — not lowball numbers. Every situation is unique, so call us and we'll give you an honest assessment.
How quickly can you close?+
We can close in as little as 7 days if needed. However, we work on your timeline — if you need more time, that's perfectly fine. You choose the closing date that works best for you.
Do I need to make repairs before selling?+
Absolutely not. We buy houses in any condition — fire damage, foundation issues, outdated systems, major repairs needed. You don't have to fix, clean, or stage anything. Sell it exactly as-is.
Are there any fees or commissions?+
Zero. No agent commissions, no closing costs, no hidden fees. The offer we make is the amount you receive at closing. What we offer is what you get.
What types of properties do you buy?+
We buy single-family homes, condos, townhomes, multi-family properties, vacant land, and commercial properties throughout all of Georgia — any condition, any price range.
What if my house is in foreclosure or I'm behind on payments?+
We can often help. We've worked with many homeowners facing foreclosure, divorce, probate, or financial hardship. The sooner you contact us, the more options we have available. Call us today.
How is this different from listing with an agent?+
When you list with an agent: you wait 3-6 months, pay 5-6% commission, make repairs, allow showings, and the deal can fall through. With us: cash offer in 24 hours, close fast, zero repairs, zero fees, guaranteed closing.
What information do I need to get started?+
Just basic property info — address, rough condition, and your situation. We'll handle the rest. There's no obligation and no pressure. Call (404) 590-1613 or fill out our form.
